Planetary and Moon texture maps: Solar System Scope / INOVE.
Source: https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/
License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Files: textures/earth.jpg, jupiter.jpg, mars.jpg, mercury.jpg, moon.jpg,
neptune.jpg, saturn.jpg, sun.jpg, uranus.jpg, venus.jpg.
Original 2K maps are rendered with lighting, exposure and, for satellite
approximations, color tints. Venus uses the atmosphere map.
No external website code or copy was incorporated.

Reference physical parameters:
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/phys_par.html
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/astro_par.html

That data set in turn packages Hipparcos/Tycho astrometry and Axel Mellinger's
all-sky survey isophotes. Conversion script: tools/build-sky.mjs.

Planetary ephemeris: JPL Solar System Dynamics, "Keplerian Elements for
Approximate Positions of the Major Planets" (1800-2050 fit).
https://ssd.jpl.nasa.gov/planets/approx_pos.html

Third-party software: Three.js (MIT), Vite (MIT).

2026-09-07 visible-light appearance audit:
Original illustration maps remain unchanged on disk. Sun map is no longer
rendered; white photosphere replaces its false-color appearance. Venus,
Uranus and Neptune use documented approximate visible-light display profiles;
Mars saturation is reduced. These are not spectrally calibrated products.
Sources and limitations: COLOR-AUDIT.md in the source repository.
